AGIWM Flyer Studio – Privacy

Amazing God International Women's Ministries (AGIWM) · Last updated 13 November 2026

AGIWM Flyer Studio (flyers.soundmind.org.in) is a free tool the ministry uses to make weekly prayer meeting flyers.

Google Drive

If you choose From Drive, the studio asks Google for permission to open only the files you pick in the Google Drive picker (the drive.file permission). It cannot see, list or change any other file in your Drive.

A picked image is downloaded once into your browser, made smaller, and placed on the flyer. The studio does not keep your Google sign-in after you close the page, and it does not send your Google data anywhere else. Your Google account details are not stored.

The studio's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What is stored

Flyers you are working on are saved in your own browser. If you connect the studio to the ministry's live flyer, the current flyer (text and images) is kept on the ministry's server so Claude and the team can work on it together. Nothing is sold or shared with advertisers.

Removing access

You can remove the studio's Drive access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
